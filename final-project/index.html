One Month of Social Media Management: Amihan Kitchen

Your job isn't simply to make posts. Understand the brief, make reasonable plans, create or coordinate content, respect approval, process community interactions, review performance, and recommend next steps.

The client brief

Amihan Kitchen is a fictional small-batch Filipino pantry and gift-box business. It sells curated, shelf-stable food products inspired by different regions and stories of Filipino food culture. It sells online and wants its social channels to help people discover the brand, understand the products, and feel connected to the stories behind them.

Primary audienceAdults interested in Filipino food, regional food culture, gifting, and convenient pantry products.
Business goalsIncrease relevant awareness, drive qualified product-page traffic, strengthen community interaction.
Primary platformsFacebook and Instagram.
Content capacityApproximately 12 core posts per month, plus routine community activity.
Brand personalityWarm, grounded, curious, welcoming, informative; avoid exaggerated hype.
Content pillarsFilipino food knowledge; product value; regional stories; behind the scenes; community/customer conversation.
Primary CTA directionLearn more, visit product information, ask a question, save/share useful information, or explore the collection when appropriate.
Approval ruleDrafts must be approved by the client before scheduling. Sensitive claims, customer stories, complaints, and rights questions require client review.
RestrictionsDo not invent health claims, testimonials, awards, statistics, or product attributes. Do not publish customer images without appropriate permission.
VA boundaryThe VA can plan, draft, coordinate, schedule approved content, handle routine interactions using approved information, document issues, and recommend tests. Business decisions remain with the client.
