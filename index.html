Social Media Management VA — Beginner Foundations

Learn the foundations of Social Media Management VA work through realistic client briefs, content planning, publishing workflows, community situations, analytics, AI-assisted tasks, quality checks, and a one-month simulated client project.

What this training is for

This training is for aspiring VAs who are new to Social Media Management or want a structured beginner foundation. Prior marketing, customer-facing, Canva, or social media experience can help, but is not required.

The goal is not to become a graphic designer or influencer. The goal is to understand and practice the operational work that helps a client's social media stay useful, organized, consistent, responsive, and measurable.

The core SMM workflow, used throughout this training:
Understand → Research → Plan → Create/Coordinate → Review → Approve → Schedule → Publish → Monitor → Engage → Report → Improve

What this training prepares you to understand

  • How SMM VA work fits into a client's broader social media workflow
  • How to understand a client, brand, audience, goals, and instructions before creating content
  • How to plan content around pillars, formats, platforms, and business objectives
  • How to build and maintain a practical content calendar and approval workflow
  • How to write basic captions, hooks, and calls-to-action
  • How to coordinate simple visual content without turning into a graphic-design program
  • How to schedule and verify approved content
  • How to manage routine comments and messages, and recognize when to escalate
  • How to use social listening and analytics to find useful signals
  • How to use AI as an assistant while keeping human review, accuracy, brand voice, and accountability
  • How to protect client information, respect content rights, and quality-check your own work

What this course is not

  • Not a Canva mastery course
  • Not a graphic-design certification
  • Not an influencer-growth course
  • Not a promise of followers, clients, income, or employment
  • Not advanced paid advertising or strategy consulting
  • Not a substitute for a real client's brand guidelines, policies, or platform documentation

Each lesson follows the same sequence — Learn → Observe → Practice → Check → Apply. Take your time with the exercises. A correct answer matters less than being able to explain why an action is appropriate, what evidence supports it, and when a client decision is required.
